# The World Ledger — Thursday, 19 November 2026

A broadsheet front page titled **THE WORLD LEDGER**, dated **Thursday, 19 November 2026**.

The front page opens with a photorealistic photograph of the first trilateral summit of the
United States, China and Russia — Trump, Xi and Putin — in Shenzhen, paired with a caption bar
over the hero. Beneath sit the lead headline, the deck, a red news ticker, and three secondary
columns.

## Requirements

- The composed `entry.png` is a 1024x1024 PNG under 1 MiB.
- `main.py` is deterministic, makes no network calls, and copies the entry to the output
  directory.

## Run

    python3 compose19.py
    MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/output python3 main.py
